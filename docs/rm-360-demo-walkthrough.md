# RM 360 Demo Walkthrough (30 Minutes)

## Objective

Demonstrate a relationship-manager-facing, agentic 360 customer view that:

- surfaces proactive risks before conversation starts,
- recommends next-best actions and products,
- enables natural-language customer questioning with explainable outputs,
- adapts by customer context (Sarah Chen vs Mohammed Ali).

## Audience

- Banking client stakeholders
- Frontline operations leaders
- RM / teller supervisors
- Technology and data teams

## Demo Setup

- Logged in as: `relationship_manager`
- Demo page: RM Workspace 360
- Demo customers:
  - Sarah Chen
  - Mohammed Ali

## Success Criteria

By the end of the demo, stakeholders should clearly see:

1. A single, actionable 360 view for frontline teams
2. Proactive issue detection
3. Distinct customer-specific recommendations
4. Explainable copilot answers (recommendation, reason, evidence, confidence)

## Time-Boxed Script

## 0:00-3:00 — Framing

Talking points:

- "When a customer walks in, frontline teams need instant context, not fragmented systems."
- "This RM 360 experience gives proactive issue flags, opportunities, and guided actions."
- "We will show the same workflow for two customers and observe context-specific outcomes."

## 3:00-8:00 — Open RM 360 and orient

Actions:

- Open RM Workspace.
- Show customer selector panel with Sarah and Mohammed.
- Select Sarah by default.

Talking points:

- "This is the consolidated 360: identity, financial snapshot, prioritized risks, opportunities, and next actions."
- "Priority ranking ensures the team knows what to discuss first."

## 8:00-13:00 — Sarah Chen storyline

Actions:

- Keep Sarah selected.
- Review sections top to bottom:
  - Header and relationship context
  - Financial snapshot
  - Prioritized risks/issues
  - Prioritized opportunities
  - Recommended next actions

Talking points:

- "For Sarah, we lead with service reassurance and optimization."
- "Current narrative: transfer friction + unusual-activity reassurance + premium optimization opportunities."
- "This is proactive preparation before the customer explains the problem."

## 13:00-18:00 — Copilot with Sarah

Actions:

- In Relationship Copilot, ask:
  - "What should I discuss first with this customer today?"
  - "What is the highest-priority risk and why?"
  - "What product should I recommend now and how should I position it?"

Talking points:

- "Responses are explainable and structured: recommendation, reason, evidence, confidence."
- "This is not generic chat; it is customer-scoped guidance grounded in profile context."

## 18:00-23:00 — Switch to Mohammed Ali

Actions:

- Switch customer selector from Sarah to Mohammed.
- Revisit the same 360 sections.

Talking points:

- "Notice the full 360 recomputes on customer switch without leaving the page."
- "Narrative shifts from optimization to stability-first: account protection and liquidity support."
- "This proves context adaptation, not static templates."

## 23:00-27:00 — Copilot parity test (same question, different answer)

Actions:

- Ask the same 2-3 questions used for Sarah.

Talking points:

- "Same RM, same interface, same prompts, but materially different recommendation and evidence."
- "This is the core of the agentic value proposition for frontline teams."

## 27:00-30:00 — Business close

Talking points:

- "Operationally: faster prep, better first-contact quality, fewer reactive escalations."
- "Commercially: improved relevance of product conversations from existing client base."
- "This aligns directly with your ask: 360 visibility, proactive issue handling, and horizontal revenue opportunities."

## Suggested Q&A Handling

- If asked about model behavior:
  - "Demo uses deterministic storyline logic with explainable outputs; production can evolve toward policy-governed model decisioning."
- If asked about compliance:
  - "Response structure is explainability-first, designed for auditability and frontline trust."
- If asked about rollout:
  - "Start with RM workflow, then extend to teller/customer-service surfaces using the same context model."

## Appendix: Reusable Prompt Set

- "What should I discuss first with this customer today?"
- "What is the highest-priority risk and why?"
- "What product should I recommend now and how should I position it?"
